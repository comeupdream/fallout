### The lean build — what changed and why it's safe

- **Fuel halved to a 3-month bank (−$650):** the foam-board warm room, the
  cold layer (a −18°C bag means the heater sleeps OFF), and the calendar carry
  the back half — months 4–6 of a winter start are March–May.
- **Medical de-Rx'd (−$410):** no prescriptions needed per decision; the
  physician-sourced antibiotic kit stays as a † flag only.
- **Stainless gravity filter → DIY dual-bucket + 2 ceramic candles (−$370):**
  same function; Sawyers, LifeStraws, tablets, pool shock all stay.
- **Solar + power banks cut (−$330):** bulk lithium primaries + crank radio.
- **Armor deferred (−$1,300):** IIIA soft ~$600/pair is the re-entry when ready.
- **Rain catchment + off-ramp deferred to spring (−$370 now):** month-4+ tools.
- **Gear trims (−$250):** one multitool; no thermoses/goggles; stove counted in heat.
- **Clothing UP $330 on purpose:** real merino ×2 sets each + proper insulated
  parkas — capital that never expires and substitutes for propane.
- **Never cut:** CO/smoke/extinguishers, the daily canned-meat protein line,
  water treatment chemicals, the free 72-hour dry run.
